Interaction Field Assignment

September 23, 2026 · Week 4

Picked the Sweetwater in the Michigan Union. Observed interactions, the system, and the place. Documented evidence + took field notes.

How I started

I wanted a place where there were multiple elements of a system that could be observed. I landed on Sweetwater because I have experienced their system before, and wanted to see how others experience it. I sat down in the space and took observations for ~ 1 hour.

Glimpse of Field notes, Obbservations, and Evidence

  • The waiting area is kind of undefined, very limited signage on where to wait
  • Group of people who were not familiar with the space, and thought you pickup your order where you placed your order
  • People hover around the pickup area, leaning against poles or sitting in the grey seats
  • People who sit in the grey seats usually are waiting for their orders and not sitting there for a prolonged period of time
  • Most people walk in from the right side (near main lobby)
  • They walk right past the self checkout kiosks and order straight at the counter
  • Most people walk to the counter because the menu is laid out on 4 screens, so its easy to see all the options available at once
